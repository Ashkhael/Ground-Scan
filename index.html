Ground Scan

Hold your phone about 2 to 3 feet above the ground and sweep slowly. Boxes mark spots that could be arrowheads, worked stone, or interesting rocks.

Detections are estimates and can be wrong. Natural rocks often look like arrowheads, so always check in hand. Collecting artifacts is restricted on public, state, and tribal land, and you need permission on private land.
